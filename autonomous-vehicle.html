Camera-LiDAR Autonomous Driving and Perpendicular Parking on a Modified Ride-On Vehicle | 2026.05 ~ 2026.07 | Silver Prize, SKKU AI Autonomous Driving Competition, Software Division

A ride-on toy car that drives and parks itself

Fitted with a camera, a LiDAR, a laptop, and an Arduino, the car completed missions from lane driving to perpendicular parking. Replay both missions on a true-scale model of the competition track.

Visual perception, decision logicSimulation validationTeam of 5Laptop CPU, no GPU
Scripted replay, not live inference | Track measured at 15.1 x 10.4 m
Lane driving: segment the lane, warp to a bird's-eye view, compute steering

The lane is segmented in the front camera image, and a cropped region of interest is warped to a bird's-eye view to set the target steering angle along the lane center. Everything ran on a laptop CPU without a GPU.

Perpendicular parking: make parking lines look like lanes and reuse the lane model

The rear camera view of the parking lines is filled with gray and given road-texture noise so that it resembles lane-driving data. The existing lane model fits the parking lines with no new training.
