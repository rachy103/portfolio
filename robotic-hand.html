16-DoF Modular Robotic Hand with Linkage-Coupled Fingers and Passive Compliant Fingertips | 2026.05 ~ 2026.09 | Bronze Prize, KSME National Student Design Competition

A 16-DoF robotic hand driven by 16 servos

Off-the-shelf servos with their housings removed sit inside each joint, and the fingertip joint is coupled to the middle joint through a linkage. Try the pose buttons or the per-finger sliders. The fingertip joint follows through the linkage without its own actuator.
